Documents become native UI.

Define interface structure and behavior once in JSON. Milano validates it and renders it through your own SwiftUI and Compose components, without owning your design system or requiring a backend. Validated at one gate, rendered identically on both platforms. Built for remotely configurable banners, interstitials, and forms.
